# Product decisions — frozen for hackathon MVP

- The product is a **local merchant discovery and ARG experience**, not a speed-based cash hunt.
- Basic quest should be solvable in ~3–5 minutes; hints available after 3 and 5 minutes.
- Everyone receives the same fixed base reward for a verified completion, regardless of skill or hints.
- ARG is **optional** and unlocks personal badges + one merchant-specific conditional secret-menu demo coupon, not extra cash.
- Coupon example: receive a fictional secret-menu item at no additional charge when purchasing at least KRW 20,000 at the same store. This is a **demo example**, not a real offer.
- No 'first discoverer', fastest person, or competitive race incentives.
- Merchants nominate clues and target objects. Operator checks playability and customer-safe location.
- In-app camera and consented location can assist verification; they cannot prove perfect presence.
- Devnet test assets and fictional storefront only during hackathon.
- Build product first; after working E2E, consider advanced fraud controls and money-handling requirements.
