Solana × MagicBlock · real-world ARG

The city is the level.

ARG Merchant Quest turns an independent shop's own story into a short, real-world mystery. Players follow public clues, walk into the shop, photograph a merchant-approved object and — once a person verifies it — receive a fixed reward on Solana Devnet. Every verified completion is recorded on a MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollup, and a second on-chain counter decides what the AI companion, Mara, is allowed to discuss.

How it works

One trail, five hands

1 · Merchant writes

Shop facts become a trail: public posts, an in-store observation, a counter question and one object to photograph. The AI Story Lab drafts concepts; nothing publishes without approval.

2 · Operator approves

A person checks playability, a customer-safe spot and honest reward and coupon terms before the trail goes live.

3 · Player explores

Clues open one at a time, the in-store half unlocks at the counter, answers are checked on the server, and a person reviews the photo.

4 · Fixed reward

A verified completion pays the same 1.00 tUSDC (valueless Devnet token) to everyone and increments the campaign counter on a MagicBlock ER.

5 · Deeper mystery

An optional clue hides in a signature product that staff release by hand (a labelled simulation, no payment). Each handoff increments a second on-chain counter.

Two counters, one program. Each campaign owns two PDAs of the same deployed program. Both are delegated to a MagicBlock ER validator, incremented on the rollup and committed back to Devnet by the validator.

The rollup gates the AI. On every page and companion request the server reads the clue milestone live from the ER (2.5 s timeout). At two or more eligible handoffs a prewritten shared story record unlocks for Mara; below that, or if the ER can't be read, it stays locked and is never sent to the model.

Honest scope. The counter records that this app verified a completion or a handoff. It does not prove physical presence, and the shared record is story text, not other players' data.

Honest limitations

What this is — and isn't

P3 native payments · experimental

Paying for AI inference with MagicBlock-native eSPL tokens is implemented on a separate branch but blocked at deposit settlement verification: a real 0.20 tUSDC rollup deposit reached the shuttle account but was not credited, and both recovery instructions fail. It is not merged, not deployed and not claimed as working.

Prototype scope

Devnet and valueless tokens only. One process and one SQLite file. A shared admin passphrase, no user accounts. Demo-grade rate limits and caps. One hot demo treasury.

Simulations are labelled

Products, orders, staff handoffs and coupons are demo simulations with no payment or POS. Photos and location are reviewed by a person and are not proof of presence.

Not yet tested

A durable public deployment, physical iOS/Android camera use, and live Claude on a public host. Live Claude Opus 5.5 was verified in the project's own production-mode runs.
